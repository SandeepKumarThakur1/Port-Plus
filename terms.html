2. Our Services

Zero Detention provides data-driven Port State Control (PSC) intelligence using historical inspection records, statistical methods and machine learning.

Our services include but not limited to :

  • Port Stringency Index: An assessment of how strict PSC inspections tend to be at a particular port.
  • Most Likely Findings: An estimate of the deficiencies most likely to be recorded for a particular vessel at a destination port.
  • Ship PSC Scorecard: An assessment of a vessel's PSC performance compared with vessels of similar characteristics.
  • Operator PSC Scorecard: An assessment of an operator's fleet PSC performance relative to comparable vessels.
  • Related reports, dashboards, messaging services, subscriptions and API integrations.

Services may be delivered through our website, WhatsApp, Telegram, email, dashboards or other supported channels.

The features and access included in each purchase are described on the relevant product page or at checkout.

4. Nature and Limitations of PSC Intelligence

Zero Detention provides statistical assessments, not guarantees of inspection outcomes.

Our analyses are based on available historical inspection records, vessel characteristics, statistical methods and machine-learning models.

Although we make reasonable efforts to maintain reliable analytical methods, PSC inspections are influenced by factors that cannot always be predicted.

Accordingly:

  • A favourable PSC score does not guarantee that a vessel will pass an inspection.
  • A low predicted risk does not guarantee that a vessel will avoid deficiencies or detention.
  • A high-risk score does not establish that a vessel is unsafe or non-compliant.
  • Predicted deficiencies are statistical estimates, not confirmed defects onboard.
  • Past inspection performance does not guarantee future performance.
  • Scores and rankings may change as additional information becomes available or analytical methods are updated.

Zero Detention does not guarantee the prevention of deficiencies, detentions or any particular regulatory or commercial outcome.

Our assessments are not official findings of regulatory non-compliance, seaworthiness, professional competence or management quality.

Zero Detention is not a Port State Control authority, flag administration, classification society or certification body. Our reports are not regulatory approvals, statutory certificates or substitutes for official inspections.

Customers remain responsible for their own operational, regulatory, technical and commercial decisions.
