1. Introduction

Zero Detention is operated by Aazadi LLC, a company registered in Wyoming, United States.

We respect your privacy and are committed to handling your personal information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

It applies to the Zero Detention website, Port State Control intelligence services, reports, dashboards, messaging services and other related products.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to provide our services, process payments, communicate with customers and maintain our platform.

Information you provide

Depending on how you use our services, we may collect:

  • Name, company name and contact details.
  • Email address and WhatsApp or Telegram contact information.
  • Vessel IMO numbers, destination ports and other vessel particulars submitted for analysis.
  • Enquiries, messages and communications with us.
  • Subscription details, registered vessels and service preferences.

Payment information

Payments are handled by third-party payment processors.

We receive information necessary to confirm and manage purchases, including transaction references, payment status, amount paid and relevant billing details.

We do not store complete payment card numbers on our servers.

Payment processors handle your payment information under their own privacy policies.

Information collected automatically

When you visit our website or use our services, we may collect:

  • IP address and approximate location derived from it.
  • Browser, device and operating system information.
  • Pages visited and basic website usage information.
  • Dates and times of service requests.
  • Technical logs, errors and security-related information.

Maritime information

Our services analyse historical Port State Control inspection records and other relevant maritime information obtained from public, official or appropriately licensed sources.

This may include vessel particulars, inspection histories, deficiencies, detentions, flag states and ship operator information.

Where maritime records contain information relating to identifiable individuals, we treat that information as personal data and handle it in accordance with applicable law.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Generate Port Stringency Index results, Most Likely Findings, Ship PSC Scorecards and Operator PSC Scorecards.
  • Deliver reports through WhatsApp, Telegram, email or our dashboard.
  • Process payments and manage service access.
  • Recognise returning customers and remember their registered vessels.
  • Maintain report history and respond to previous requests.
  • Provide customer support and respond to enquiries.
  • Send payment confirmations, service notifications and subscription expiry reminders.
  • Improve the reliability, accuracy, security and performance of our services.
  • Prevent fraud, investigate misuse and comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Machine Learning and Automated Analysis

Zero Detention uses statistical methods and machine learning to analyse historical PSC inspection data and identify patterns in vessel and port performance.

Our analytical models are operated as part of our own service. Customer requests are not sent to external AI model providers for analysis.

The models may process vessel particulars, destination ports, historical inspection information and other relevant maritime data.

The resulting scores and reports are intended to support maritime operational and commercial decision-making.

They do not constitute official Port State Control assessments, guarantees of inspection outcomes or legally binding decisions about individuals.

5. WhatsApp, Telegram and Other Communication Channels

Customers may access Zero Detention through WhatsApp, Telegram, email and our website dashboard.

When you contact us through these channels, we may process your contact identifier, message content, vessel details and service requests.

We may retain previous requests and registered vessel information to make future interactions easier.

For example, a returning customer may be able to request a new port assessment without entering the vessel's IMO number again.

WhatsApp, Telegram and other communication providers process information under their own privacy policies and terms of service.

We do not control how these independent platforms process information for their own purposes.

6. Payments and Subscriptions

We use external payment service providers to process purchases securely.

For one-time purchases, we retain the information needed to confirm payment, generate the requested report and provide customer support.

For subscription and fleet-access products, we may retain:

  • Customer and company identifiers.
  • Authorised contact information.
  • Registered vessel IMO numbers.
  • Subscription start and expiry dates.
  • Payment references and access permissions.

Where automatic renewal is available, the relevant payment and cancellation terms will be shown during checkout.

We do not require customers to create a conventional username-and-password account where our services support access through existing communication channels.

7. Website Analytics and Cookies

We use basic website analytics to understand how visitors use our website and identify opportunities for improvement.

This may include information about pages visited, general traffic patterns, devices used and website performance.

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies for essential functions, security and analytics.

Where consent is required for non-essential analytics cookies or tracking technologies, we will request it before using them.

You can manage your preferences through our website's cookie controls, where provided, and through your browser settings.

We do not use website analytics as a reason to sell personal information to advertisers.

8. Sharing Information with Service Providers

We may share limited information with trusted third-party providers where necessary to operate our services.

These may include providers of:

  • Payment processing.
  • Cloud hosting and technical infrastructure.
  • Email and messaging delivery.
  • Database storage and workflow automation.
  • Report delivery and document storage.
  • Website analytics, security and technical monitoring.

We share only information reasonably necessary for the relevant service and use appropriate contractual and security arrangements where required.

We may also disclose information when legally required or where necessary to protect our services, customers or legal rights.

9. Marketing and Service Communications

We may send communications related to purchases, reports, account access, subscription expiry and important service updates.

We may also offer maritime news, PSC updates and community communications through email, WhatsApp or other supported channels.

Marketing messages will be sent in accordance with applicable law and any required consent.

You may opt out of marketing communications at any time using the unsubscribe options provided or by contacting us.

Opting out of marketing does not prevent essential communications relating to purchases or active services.

10. Legal Basis for Processing

Where the EU GDPR, UK GDPR or similar laws apply, we process personal information on one or more of the following grounds:

Performance of a contract: To provide purchased reports, manage subscriptions, deliver intelligence and provide customer support.

Legitimate interests: To maintain service security, prevent fraud, improve platform performance and administer our business, where those interests are not overridden by individuals' rights.

Legal obligations: To comply with applicable accounting, tax, regulatory and legal requirements.

Consent: Where required for optional analytics, marketing communications or other activities that depend on your permission.

Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time without affecting processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

11. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy.

Retention periods depend on the type of information and the reason it was collected.

  • Customer and subscription records are retained while needed to provide services, support renewals and resolve account-related issues.
  • Report requests and relevant communications may be retained to support report history and customer enquiries.
  • Payment and billing records are retained for applicable accounting, tax and legal requirements.
  • Technical and security logs are retained for operational, security and troubleshooting purposes.

When personal information is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it, subject to applicable legal and technical requirements.

12. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, alteration, disclosure and misuse.

These measures include appropriate access restrictions, secure communications and controls over the systems and service providers used to operate Zero Detention.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

If you believe your information or service access has been compromised, please contact us at connect@zerodetention.com.

13. International Data Transfers

Zero Detention is operated by a company registered in the United States and serves customers internationally.

Personal information may be processed or stored in countries outside your country of residence, depending on the location of our infrastructure and service providers.

Where applicable law requires safeguards for international transfers, we will use appropriate lawful mechanisms.

You may contact us for further information about the safeguards relevant to your information.

14. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of eligible personal information.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Request a portable copy of eligible information.
  • Withdraw consent where applicable.
  • Opt out of certain uses of personal information where required by law.

To exercise these rights, email connect@zerodetention.com.

We may need to verify your identity before processing a request.

Some information may need to be retained to comply with legal obligations or for other lawful reasons.

Where applicable, you may also lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection authority.

15. Children's Privacy

Zero Detention is intended for maritime professionals, businesses and adult users.

Our services are not directed towards children under the age of 18.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through our services.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review the matter.

16. Third-Party Services

Our website and reports may contain links to third-party websites, payment providers, messaging platforms or external information sources.

These services operate under their own privacy policies.

We encourage users to review those policies before sharing personal information with third parties.

17. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our services, technology, business practices or legal obligations.
