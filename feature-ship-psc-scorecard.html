Ship PSC Scorecard

How does your ship really compare with its peers?

Fair comparisons start with similar ships.

How well has a ship really performed in Port State Control inspections?

The number of deficiencies or detentions tells part of the story. But those numbers mean much less without knowing what you're comparing them against.

Consider a 25-year-old bulk carrier regularly trading through high-scrutiny ports and a brand-new LNG carrier operating on a fixed route.

They face very different inspection conditions. Comparing their PSC records at face value would not be fair.

The more meaningful question is: How has each ship performed compared with other ships like it?

That is what the Ship PSC Scorecard does.

It compares a vessel's PSC performance against ships of similar age, type and size, while also accounting for the strictness of the ports it has visited.

Whether you're a charterer choosing between two vessels or a ship manager reviewing performance, the scorecard helps you see how a vessel actually stands among its peers, rather than relying on raw inspection numbers alone.

Adjusting for age, type, size and ports.

A vessel's PSC record is influenced by several factors beyond its actual inspection performance. Its age, type, size and the ports where it trades can all affect the number and nature of deficiencies recorded.

To make a fairer comparison, our machine learning models benchmark each vessel against a peer group of ships with similar characteristics, primarily age, ship type and size.

It also accounts for the strictness of the ports visited. This ensures that a vessel trading regularly through high-scrutiny ports is not automatically rated worse than a comparable ship operating in less stringent regions.

By combining these factors, the model produces a peer-adjusted PSC score that shows how a vessel has performed relative to similar ships under different inspection conditions. The same approach works across regions such as the Paris MoU and Tokyo MoU.

The result is a more meaningful assessment of a vessel's PSC performance, helping charterers, owners and managers make comparisons that go beyond simple deficiency counts and detention records.
