Operator PSC Scorecard

How does an operator's fleet perform compared with fleets of similar vessels?

Two fleets. Very different ships.

Imagine two ship operators.

One manages 20 brand-new container ships. The other manages 20 aging bulk carriers.

The first operator will probably record fewer PSC deficiencies. Looking at the total numbers, it might appear that the first operator is doing a much better job.

But is that necessarily true?

The second operator could have an excellent maintenance team, managing a much older fleet under far more demanding conditions.

Simply comparing the total deficiencies or detentions across their fleets would not give us a fair picture.

That is what the Operator PSC Scorecard addresses.

It compares each vessel in an operator's fleet against ships of similar age, type and size, while also accounting for the strictness of the ports where those vessels have been inspected.

For charterers, insurers and owners, this provides a fairer way to compare operators, regardless of the age or composition of their fleets.

Comparing fleets on equal terms.

Comparing the PSC performance of two operators is not straightforward when their fleets differ significantly in age, ship type, size and trading patterns.

An operator managing newer vessels may naturally record fewer deficiencies than one managing an older fleet. Similarly, operators whose vessels regularly visit high-scrutiny ports may accumulate more findings than those trading in less stringent regions.

Our machine learning models account for these differences by evaluating every vessel in an operator's fleet against its own peer group.

Each ship is benchmarked against vessels of similar age, type and size, using historical PSC inspection records across major MoU regions, including the Paris MoU and Tokyo MoU. The model also adjusts for differences in port stringency, so vessels trading through stricter ports are assessed in the context of the inspection conditions they face.

These peer-adjusted vessel scores are then combined to calculate an overall PSC performance score for the operator.

By accounting for fleet composition and port strictness, the model allows operators with very different fleets to be compared on a more consistent basis.

The result is an Operator PSC Scorecard that shows how an operator's fleet has performed relative to comparable vessels, giving charterers, insurers and owners a more meaningful basis for evaluating its PSC track record.
